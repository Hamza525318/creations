## 1. Executive Summary & Link Philosophy

Link building for CREATION'S focuses on **topical relevance** (interior design, textiles, window treatments) and **local geographical authority** (Chennai, specifically Besant Nagar / South Chennai). 

Rather than chasing low-quality spam links, our strategy focuses on 5 high-authority pillars:
1. **Authoritative Local Citations & Directories** (NAP Consistency)
2. **Trade Partnerships with Chennai Architects & Interior Designers**
3. **Linkable Asset Promotion** (Window Measurement Guide)
4. **Hyperlocal South Chennai Media & Neighborhood Portals**
5. **Supplier & Brand Backlinks**

2. **Project Portfolio Credits**: When CREATION'S fabricates and installs curtains or blinds for a designer’s project, both parties publish the case study with reciprocal links.
